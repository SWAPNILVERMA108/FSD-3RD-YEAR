About the College

Two decades of outcome-focused learning

Founded in 2000 under a not-for-profit educational society, this engineering college has grown from a single campus in Ghaziabad into one of the region's recognised names in technical education. Approved by AICTE and affiliated to a leading state technical university, it offers a full spread of undergraduate and postgraduate programmes shaped around industry relevance.

The institution's culture rests on three ideas: rigorous fundamentals, hands-on project work, and constant exposure to real-world problems — through internships, incubation support and a large network of recruiting partners that return to campus every year.
